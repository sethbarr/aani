Profile activity across the panel

Does chemistry associated with ant plant selection inhibit only the cultivated fungus, or does activity extend to other fungal lineages and growth forms?

This is an exploratory activity panel. Testing these selected compounds does not establish that ant avoidance improves discovery. That requires a separate, matched comparison with accepted-plant candidates.

Starting materials and evidence

What a useful result would tell us

Activity in the cultivar alone supports a narrower ecological effect. Activity across several validated assays motivates follow-up on the tested panel. Neither outcome establishes clinical efficacy, fungal killing, or universal broad-spectrum activity.

Keep raw measurements, assay versions, isolate identities, controls and uncertainty. Confirm promising findings with independent runs and additional isolates before generalizing.

Diversity with a clear purpose

The proposed panel spans yeast and filamentous growth forms, with an ant cultivar as the ecological reference. Each organism needs its own suitable method, readout and schedule.

An intact garden contains a community. An identified cultivar isolate gives a more interpretable first test. Additional donor-colony isolates would strengthen later replication.
