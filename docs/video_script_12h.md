**[0:00–0:12] Problem**

When a leafcutter colony refuses a plant, that refusal is a chemical assay run by
evolution against a live fungus. Fifty years of those observations sit in the
literature, and no screening library contains them.

**[0:12–0:26] Solution**

aani makes them computable. Papers in, grounded behavioural observations out,
resolved to plant names, joined to chemical structures and antifungal assay data,
with a source anchor at every link. Analysis plan frozen in a commit before any
outcome was seen.

**[0:26–0:48] What we found**

It runs end to end. Two results. Quote-grounding passed 41 records; source review
kept 9 — grounding checks provenance, not meaning. And of 2,183 mapped structures,
24 have an antifungal measurement. One percent. The primary test is non-estimable,
and we can now say exactly why.

**[0:48–1:00] Next 12 hours**

Grounding that survives tables, to close the gap between what the pipeline found
and what a careful reader found. Then the coverage funnel through to genera.
