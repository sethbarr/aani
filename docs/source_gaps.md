## What each source can contribute

### Saverschek 2010: priority for direct natural-leaf choice

The paper's indexed text describes field tests of ten seldom-harvested species
with *Atta colombica*. Colonies from habitat containing those species initially
avoided them; colonies from habitat without them initially accepted the leaves,
then avoided four species at later tests. This is direct choice evidence beyond
absence from a diet list. The study also includes a separate cycloheximide
manipulation. Preserve habitat, previous exposure, time and treatment context;
do not label every experiment natural because some leaves were untreated at the
final test. Under the frozen conservative aggregation, genuine acceptance and
rejection within a genus can create a conflict rather than a rejected genus.
These design facts are verified from the [paper text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/248591945_Avoiding_plants_unsuitable_for_the_symbiotic_fungus_learning_and_long-term_memory_in_leaf-cutting_ants).

### Crumière 2021: natural acceptance, with source tables still to retrieve

The published paper studies carried plant fragments from free-ranging
*Atta colombica* and reports 44 sampled plant species. This supports acceptance
records, subject to species identity and source grounding. Low intake or missing
plants do not demonstrate rejection. [Published article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9292433/).

The Obsidian notes report 87 rows transcribed from Table S11 and 44 barcode
identities from Table S3. Those exact counts are local-note claims in this audit:
the original supplementary tables were not independently opened. Retrieve the
published supporting information or the cited Dryad files, then reconcile the
transcription and preserve table provenance. Prefer the source's barcode mapping
over treating each fragment row as an independent plant observation.

### Waller 1982: distinguish toughness from chemical avoidance

The local reading list describes leaf-disc tests separating toughness and
unpalatability. That design would be a useful semantic review case: failure to
cut an intact mature leaf is different from rejecting a provided disc. The
publisher identity is verified, but the experiment methods and named-plant
directions remain unverified here because the complete article was unavailable.
Use the user's existing PDF if it can be located; do not promote the note into
primary evidence.

### Hubbell 1984: separate leaf extracts from leaf choice

The [published abstract](https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2307/1938314)
verifies a 42-species *Atta cephalotes* repellency survey using leaf extracts. It
also reports correspondence with leaf-preference tests and field selection.
This qualifies the reading-list claim that it contains an explicit rejected set:
the extract screen alone is not an eligible natural-substrate choice table under
version 1. Retrieve and inspect the actual natural-leaf tests and their original
citations before deciding which observations are primary. Preserve seasonal
context and avoid counting a cited leaf-choice dataset twice.

### Herz 2008: treatment-context validation and possible untreated controls

The [full paper](https://repository.si.edu/bitstreams/b4e2196b-974b-46b8-b5ee-c7c956d40dac/download)
uses *Acromyrmex lundi*, with privet, rose and bramble in paired leaf-disc tests.
Its experimental rejection is induced by cycloheximide infiltration; some later
tests use untreated leaves after that induced experience. Those contexts must
remain traceable and cannot establish naturally unsuitable plant chemistry.
Untreated control choices may yield acceptance records after review. Table 1
and the treatment methods provide a compact test of whether extraction preserves
natural controls versus experimentally induced rejection.

### Wirth 2003: harvest coverage and availability, not inferred rejection

The local notes derive 33 “never harvested” entries by comparing a forest
inventory against the harvest list, alongside 49 harvested and three ambiguous
matches. The chapter identities above are independently verified; those table
contents and counts are not independently verified in this audit. Retrieve
Chapters 4, 10 and 11 to ground harvest records and evaluate availability.
The inventory-minus-harvest complement remains a review/query seed under the
frozen exclusion of rejection inferred solely from absence. A size or abundance
adjustment does not turn absence into an observed choice assay.
