## Morais2015 — publisher version still pending

**Extracts of Ageratum conyzoides, Coriandrum sativum and Mentha piperita inhibit the growth of the symbiotic fungus of leaf-cutting ants.** Industrial Crops and Products65:463–466. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.indcrop.2014.10.054).

The [UFV repository record](https://locus.ufv.br/items/c5c19be4-5ecf-44d1-ad24-1509cf280d52/full) lists a PDF, but its download route led to a repository login page. The publisher page was not accessible in full.

**Available substitute:** Morais2012, *Extratos botânicos e seus efeitos em Atta sexdens rubropilosa*, UFV master’s dissertation. [Saved PDF](https://locus.ufv.br/server/api/core/bitstreams/da53d5c4-077b-4210-8036-f6bda05b963c/content) / [institutional PDF](https://locus.ufv.br/server/api/core/bitstreams/da53d5c4-077b-4210-8036-f6bda05b963c/content). Chapter2 methods and Table 1 were checked; percentages match the journal abstract.

**Why the article still matters:** the thesis describes 25/50/100 mg/mL stocks followed by1:10 dilution into medium, while its table retains the stock labels. The publisher version or author clarification could resolve the actual assay dose. No need to retrieve the thesis again.
